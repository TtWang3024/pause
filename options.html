Breaks

A break is a short, deliberate rest after your free time ends, so a session closes on a calm note instead of trailing off. When it is on, you commit to a break length up front on a small screen, and it always opens at the maximum so you actively choose a shorter break each time.

Ending a break early

Every break carries a hold-to-return button, a way back by choice. Ending early is a choice, not a failure: the log keeps the minutes you actually rested.

Magic stars

Your "magic power" notes (thoughts, body, and mood) are captured before each session and shown as a star map on the reflection screen.

The moment before you open a distracting site is an impulse. Naming what's happening (your thoughts, where you feel it in your body, your mood) is called affect labeling. Putting a feeling into words is an evidence-based way to loosen its grip, so each session can open with a small, unhurried reflection instead of a reflex. Your notes never leave your device. Over time they gather into a private star map, where more recent notes shine brighter. No scores, no streaks, no judgement. Just your own sky to glance back at.
