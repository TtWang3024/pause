Breaks

A break is a short, deliberate rest after your free time ends, so a session closes on a calm note instead of trailing off. When it is on, you commit to a break length up front on a small screen: the dial offers 3, 10 or 30 minutes and opens at 3, so a longer break is a deliberate choice each time. The break ends on its own; with Magic stars on, the star it earned is shown before the sky returns.

Break screen message

Magic stars

Your notes (thoughts, body, and mood) are saved in history. Each completed experiment lights one star on the sky.

The moment before you open a distracting site is an impulse. Naming what's happening (your thoughts, where you feel it in your body, your mood) is called affect labeling. Putting a feeling into words is an evidence-based way to loosen its grip, so each session can open with a small, unhurried reflection instead of a reflex. Your notes never leave your device. Completed experiments gather into a private star map. Reflections and breaks stay in history without lighting stars. No scores, no streaks, no judgement. Just your own sky to glance back at.
